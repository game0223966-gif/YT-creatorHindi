📝 Simple Video Script

10-minute video ko yaad rakhne ke liye chhote sections mein banao.

  • Hook: pehle 5–10 seconds mein problem batao
  • Promise: viewer kya seekhega
  • 3–5 main points aur examples
  • Quick recap + natural call to action

📅 7-Day Starter Plan

  • Day 1: ek niche aur audience choose karo
  • Day 2: 10 video ideas likho
  • Day 3: ek short script banao
  • Day 4: record aur edit karo
  • Day 5: thumbnail aur title banao

Mobile Editing Tutorial Path

01 · CapCut BasicsImport clips, timeline, split, trim, delete gaps aur arrange shots.
02 · Smooth TransitionsClip boundary tap karo, transition choose karo, duration short rakho aur preview dekho.
03 · Captions & TextText → Auto captions (availability app version par depend karti hai), phir spelling manually check karo.
04 · Audio & VoiceVoice ko clear rakho, music low rakho, clipping se bacho.
05 · Color & EffectsAdjustments halka use karo; har clip ka exposure/white balance match karo.
06 · Export SettingsAam taur par 1080p aur source ke barabar frame rate choose karo.
